# Content Strategy & Brand Voice

Status: Current

## The Backfill Launch Model

Ernest has 3–5 years of existing art, photography, and cultural storytelling work. The launch strategy is a "backfill relaunch" — existing content is the inventory. No new content needs to be created before launch. The backfill is an unfair advantage: the content exists, it just needs to be uploaded and put into Printful.

## The Three-Beat Product Description Formula

Every product description follows a three-beat structure:

1. **Visual fact** — What does the viewer see? "A bold graphic print featuring a lion rendered in vibrant blocks of gold, crimson, and cobalt."
2. **Meaning** — What does it mean? "A contemporary vision of a timeless symbol of African strength and dignity."
3. **Placement suggestion** — Where does it go? "Commanding presence for a living room focal wall or office statement piece."

This structure is not a template to be filled in mechanically — it is a guide. The best descriptions feel natural, not formulaic.

## Gallery-Catalogue vs Marketplace Positioning

Kumachi Prints is a catalogue, not a marketplace. Every product is curated. There are no third-party sellers. This positioning means:
- Fewer products, each with more editorial depth
- Products are presented as artworks, not SKUs
- The browse experience is editorial (drops, stories, artist features) not algorithmic (infinite scroll, "customers also bought")
- Trust comes from curation, not from reviews

## Tagline

**"The ones worth keeping."** — This positions every print as a permanent addition to the buyer's life, not disposable decor. It signals quality, intentionality, and emotional connection.

Alternatives considered and rejected:
- "Art that demands to be seen" — too aggressive, implies confrontation
- "Stories you can hang on your wall" — too literal, lacks poetry

## Currency Decision

USD only. The primary market is diaspora buyers in USD markets. UGX-only pricing is invisible to international buyers. Dual pricing adds complexity to the variant/pricing system with minimal benefit in Phase 1.

## Launch Strategy (Pre-Broadcast)

Before any public broadcast, Ernest sends 10 personal direct messages to the most engaged social followers with a preview link. This generates initial orders, validates the checkout flow, and creates early social proof before the public announcement.

## Photography Standards

- Product images: clean, well-lit, consistent aspect ratio
- Room mockups: lifestyle shots showing the print in a real room setting (highest-converting format in art e-commerce)
- Artist portraits: editorial style, consistent lighting
- Series hero images: full-bleed, dramatic, story-telling

## SEO Content Principles

- Every product page has a unique title and meta description (Sanity SEO override takes priority)
- Every editorial page (drops, artists) has unique metadata
- No duplicate content across similar products
- Canonical URLs maintained through domain migration
- Sitemap includes all product, collection, series, artist, and page URLs
