# Project Vision & Business Context

Status: Current

## What Kumachi Prints Is

Kumachi Prints is a premium headless e-commerce storefront for selling physical art prints — digital art, photography, and cultural storytelling work produced by or curated by Kumachi. It is the commerce arm of the Kumachi Empire, a four-property creative business ecosystem built by Ernest Serunkuma (Kampala, Uganda).

The store differentiates through an editorial layer that positions it as gallery-adjacent, not a mass-market print shop. Every print has context: artist, series, technique, inspiration. Drops are curated editorial releases. The forthcoming AI Studio will offer style-locked AI print generation.

## What Kumachi Prints Is Not

- Not a commodity poster shop. Price reflects the art, not just the materials.
- Not a marketplace. Every product is curated by Kumachi.
- Not inventory-intensive. Printful handles all fulfilment — Kumachi never touches the product.

## Gallery-Adjacent Positioning

Kumachi Prints prices like art, not like posters. The perceived value comes from:
- The artist's reputation and backstory
- The limited-edition scarcity signal
- The editorial layer (story, technique, inspiration)
- The premium framing options
- The gallery-quality colour and paper standards

## The Diaspora Buyer

The core audience is diaspora buyers in USD markets who connect with African-rooted visual identity. This drives:
- **USD-only pricing** — UGX pricing is invisible to international buyers
- **International shipping** — Printful's global fulfilment network
- **Cultural storytelling** — the editorial layer is the product differentiator

## Backfill as Unfair Advantage

Ernest has 3–5 years of existing artwork, photography, and cultural storytelling work. No new content needs to be created before launch. The backfill catalogue is the launch inventory. The question is not "what will we sell" but "which existing work do we put up first."

## Revenue Model

Customer orders → Printful produces and ships the physical print → Kumachi earns the margin (retail price minus Printful base cost). No inventory held. No upfront stock cost. Retail pricing = Printful base cost × 2.5× to 4× markup.

## Brand Tone

- Gallery-adjacent, editorial, confident
- Premium but accessible — not fine art auction prices, but considered home decor
- Cultural confidence: the work does not explain itself. It is from a culture, for anyone
- "The ones worth keeping." — the working tagline
